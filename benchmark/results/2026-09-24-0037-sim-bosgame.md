`vs comp` is THE number: `torch.compile` is what somebody who cares
about speed actually runs, so it is the target and it leads the table.
`vs eager` is not a second opinion on the same question. It is there for
two narrower jobs: it is the only denominator left when a model does not
compile (a mutable-state model) or does not run eager (a driver fault),
and the SPREAD between the two columns says where a gap lives — close to
eager and far from compiled is missing fusion, far from both is kernel
quality before fusion enters.

`!` marks a row whose samples the clock gate rejected: the card idled
inside the sample, so the number is worth less than a gated one and more
than nothing.
